Thetis' entry level FIDO2 key is a durable USB-A token with a folding cover that protects the connector when not in use. It supports FIDO2 and U2F with discoverable credentials, plus OATH HOTP and TOTP, but does not include NFC.

Thetis has expanded its line considerably since this key launched. Other entries in this catalog cover:

* [Thetis Nano-A](../thetis-nano-a/) and [Nano-C](../thetis-nano-c/), low profile USB-A and USB-C variants with no NFC.
* [Thetis PRO-A](../thetis-pro-a/) and [PRO-C](../thetis-pro-c/), USB-A and USB-C with NFC.
* [Thetis PRO FIDO2](../thetis-pro-fido2/), the triple interface flagship with USB-A, USB-C, and NFC in one body.
* [Thetis PRO-C Plus](../thetis-pro-c-plus/), an aluminium shielded USB-C with NFC.
* [Thetis PRO with PinPlex](../thetis-pinplex/), the triple interface key with PIV smart card added.
* [Thetis BioFP](../thetis-fido2-bio/), a fingerprint variant.
* [Thetis BLE](../thetis-ble/), a USB-A plus NFC plus Bluetooth Low Energy variant.
