The OnlyKey DUO is a smaller variant of the [OnlyKey](../onlykey/) with both a USB-A and a USB-C connector. The feature set is the same as the original: on device PIN entry, password storage, FIDO2 and U2F, Yubico OTP, OATH TOTP and HOTP, PGP, and SSH.

The DUO is the better choice for travellers and laptop users who need to alternate between USB-A docks and USB-C ports.
