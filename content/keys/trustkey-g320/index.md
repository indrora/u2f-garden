The G320 was TrustKey's USB-C fingerprint FIDO2 key, the USB-C sibling to the [G310](../trustkey-g310/). TrustKey also sold the G320H, which added Windows Hello fingerprint support. Both have been discontinued. The successor line is the B series, with [B210](../trustkey-b210/) and [B210H](../trustkey-b210h/) covering the same use cases.
