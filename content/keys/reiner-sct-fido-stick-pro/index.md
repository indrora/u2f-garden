The FIDO Stick Pro is REINER SCT's branded FIDO key, manufactured by [Swissbit](../../vendor/swissbit/) under a 2023 partnership. Both the USB-A and USB-C models include NFC, and both support FIDO2, U2F, PIV smart card, and OATH HOTP and TOTP.

REINER SCT also sells a separate Authenticator product line, but those are TOTP only hardware tokens, not FIDO keys, and are not in scope of this catalog.
