The eSecu Bluetooth FIDO Key is one of the few currently produced FIDO2 keys with Bluetooth Low Energy. As noted in [our FAQ](/faq/), the Bluetooth security key category is essentially over after Google retired the BLE Titan in 2021. Excelsecu kept BLE in the line, primarily as an enterprise workforce option for use with paired desktops.

USB-A and Bluetooth. No NFC and no biometrics on this variant.
