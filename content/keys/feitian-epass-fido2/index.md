Feitian's ePass FIDO2 series is a wide line. Each "casing" is a different form factor:

* The A4B is a traditional "key" shape.
* The K10 sits close to flush with the USB connector, similar to the Yubikey Nano series.
* The K12 is a stick form factor.
* The K21 and K28 offer USB-C connectors in a compact form factor.
* The K39 provides a simple USB-C stick.
* The newer K40 and K45 add USB-C with NFC for users who want tap to authenticate.

Feitian is the OEM for many other vendors, with various options turned on or off for those specific users. The Google Titan keys are built on the same hardware family, with a Google specific firmware build.

For the Lightning equipped variant aimed at older iPhones, see the [iePass K44](../feitian-iepass-k44/). For the multi interface enterprise option, see the [AllinPass K33](../feitian-allinpass-k33/).
