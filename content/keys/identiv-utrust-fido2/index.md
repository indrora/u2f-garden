The uTrust FIDO2 NFC line is Identiv's entry tier FIDO key. It is sold as a USB-A plus NFC variant and a USB-C plus NFC variant, priced at around twenty nine and thirty five United States dollars respectively. The keys support FIDO2, U2F, and discoverable credentials. They do not include PIV smart card or OATH OTP.

The shop URL has moved. Identiv's physical security business is now sold under the Hirsch brand, and what used to be shop.identiv.com now redirects to shop.hirschsecure.com. The product line is unchanged.

For PIV and OATH on top of FIDO2, see [uTrust FIDO2 NFC+](../identiv-utrust-fido2-plus/). For the FIPS 140-3 Level 3 government variant, see [uTrust FIDO2 GOV](../identiv-utrust-fido2-gov/).
