The Thetis PRO FIDO2 is the flagship of the Thetis line. It carries a USB-A connector on one end, a USB-C connector on the other, and NFC for tap to authenticate, all in the same folding shell. This is unusual at the price point. Most three interface keys cost two to three times as much.

It supports FIDO2, U2F, discoverable credentials, and OATH HOTP and TOTP. There are no PIV or biometric features. For PIV on this body, see [PRO with PinPlex](../thetis-pinplex/). For lower cost USB-A or USB-C only variants, see the [Nano-A](../thetis-nano-a/) and [Nano-C](../thetis-nano-c/).
