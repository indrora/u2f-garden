The ATKey.Card NFC packages AuthenTrend's biometric FIDO2 stack into a credit card form factor. It carries a fingerprint sensor, NFC for tap to authenticate, and MIFARE Type A for badge access dual use. AuthenTrend also sells the ATKey.Badge, a USB-C reader released in 2025 that adds a wired connection for the card.

The card form factor competes with [GoTrust IdemCard](../gotrust-idemcard/) but with biometrics added. Fingerprint enrolment is performed over the badge accessory or through host platform WebAuthn flows.
