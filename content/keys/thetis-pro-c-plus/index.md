The Thetis PRO-C Plus is an aluminium shielded variant of the [PRO-C](../thetis-pro-c/). The product page advertises 200 discoverable credential slots and 50 OATH credential slots, considerably above what most consumer keys at this price tier offer.

Thetis labels this product as FIDO2 L2 in the title but the body of the product page lists it as FIDO2 L1. Without confirmation from a FIDO Alliance certificate, this catalog does not tag a specific Authenticator Certification Level for this key.
