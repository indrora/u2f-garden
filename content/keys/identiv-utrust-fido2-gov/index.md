The uTrust FIDO2 GOV is the variant of the Identiv and Hirsch line targeted at United States federal agencies and contractors. It is FIPS 140-3 Level 3 validated and meets the NIST SP 800-63B AAL3 guidance for phishing resistant authenticators in federal use. The GOV variant is sold as USB-A (model 905601-1) and USB-C (model 905602-1) at around sixty five United States dollars.

This is one of two FIDO security keys in the catalog with FIPS 140-3 Level 3 validation. The other is the [Swissbit iShield Key 2 Pro FIPS](../swissbit-ishield-key-2-pro-fips/).
