The G310 was TrustKey's USB-A fingerprint FIDO2 key. TrustKey was the first vendor to ship FIDO2 Level 2 certified biometric keys, and the G series was the original line. The vendor product page now lists the entire G series (G310, G320, G310H, G320H) as not available, with the [B210](../trustkey-b210/) and [B210H](../trustkey-b210h/) replacing them. Existing G series keys continue to work.
