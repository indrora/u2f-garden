The B210 is TrustKey's current generation USB-A fingerprint key, replacing the older [G310](../trustkey-g310/). It carries FIDO2 Level 2 certification, supports discoverable credentials, and stores OATH HOTP and TOTP credentials through the TrustKey management application.

For the variant with Windows Hello support, see [B210H](../trustkey-b210h/).
