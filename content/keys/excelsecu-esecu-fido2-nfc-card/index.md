The eSecu FIDO2 NFC Card is Excelsecu's credit card form factor FIDO2 key. It supports FIDO2 over NFC tap to authenticate. There is no USB connector and no biometrics.

This is a competitor to the [GoTrust IdemCard](../gotrust-idemcard/) and the [AuthenTrend ATKey.Card](../authentrend-atkey-card/) at a lower price point. Excelsecu's pricing reflects their volume manufacturing position.
