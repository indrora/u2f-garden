The HyperFIDO Pro Titanium is the keychain sized USB-A variant of the HyperFIDO Pro line. It is the same protocol stack as the [Pro Mini](../hyperfido-pro-mini/) but in a larger metal shell with a keyring loop. For USB-C, see [Pro USB-C](../hyperfido-pro-usbc/). For NFC, see [Pro NFC](../hyperfido-pro-nfc/). For biometrics, see [Pro Bio](../hyperfido-pro-bio/).
