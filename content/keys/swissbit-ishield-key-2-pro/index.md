The iShield Key 2 Pro is Swissbit's current generation flagship. It supports FIDO2, U2F, PIV smart card, OATH HOTP and TOTP, and is available in USB-A plus NFC and USB-C plus NFC. It is manufactured in Berlin.

Swissbit positions the Pro line at organisations preparing for European compliance regimes including NIS2, DORA, and the Cyber Resilience Act, where the made in Europe story matters for procurement. RSA distributes the same hardware as the RSA iShield Key 2 in their channels.

For the variant with a MIFARE DESFire EV3 chip for physical access dual use, see [iShield Key 2 Pro MIFARE](../swissbit-ishield-key-2-pro-mifare/). For the FIPS 140-3 Level 3 validated variant, see [iShield Key 2 Pro FIPS](../swissbit-ishield-key-2-pro-fips/).
