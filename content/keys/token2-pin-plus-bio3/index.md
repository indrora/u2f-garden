The PIN+Bio3 takes the Token2 PIN+ family and adds an on device fingerprint sensor for FIDO2 user verification. PIN complexity enforcement and the discoverable credential capacity carry over from the rest of the line, so you get fingerprint, PIN, and a high credential count in one device.

Available in USB-A and USB-C. Fingerprint enrolment is handled through the Token2 management application.
