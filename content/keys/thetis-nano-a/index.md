The Thetis Nano-A is a low profile USB-A nub that sits close to flush with the port. It is the right pick for a desktop or laptop where you want a key permanently inserted rather than carried on a keyring. There is no NFC and no folding cover.

For the USB-C equivalent, see [Nano-C](../thetis-nano-c/).
