The PinPlex variant of the [PRO FIDO2](../thetis-pro-fido2/) keeps the triple interface body and adds PIV smart card support. Thetis brands its PIN handling as PinPlex. The branding refers to a PIN policy feature, not a physical PIN pad on the device.

Windows Hello for Business is supported only with Microsoft Entra ID per Thetis' product page. The device's reported AAGUID is `a3975549-b191-fd67-b8fb-017e2917fdb3`.
