The uTrust FIDO2 NFC+ extends the [uTrust FIDO2](../identiv-utrust-fido2/) with PIV smart card, OATH HOTP, and OATH TOTP. It is aimed at organisations that already have smart card based logon flows and want to consolidate onto one device. OTP credentials are managed through the Windows only uTrust Key Manager application.

USB-A and USB-C variants are both sold, at around thirty nine and forty four United States dollars respectively.
